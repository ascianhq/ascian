Choose the alternative by constraint

Use Kling 2.1 Pro as the nearest route on rate, Runway Gen-4 when the attempt budget matters most, and Veo or Hailuo when the output justifies a higher listed rate. Higgsfield is often named for camera-led exploration but has no gateway route today, so it has to be bought from Higgsfield directly.

02

Measure total generations, not duration

Count every attempt, including variations and discards, because generations are the unit the gateway charges for and clip length is not. The cheapest listed model may still not create the lowest-cost finished scene.

Seedance alternatives FAQ

What models are similar to Seedance?

Kling 2.1 Pro is the closest listed alternative on rate, with Runway Gen-4 lower and Veo and Hailuo higher. Higgsfield is often mentioned in the same shortlist but is not routable through Ascian today.

Is Hailuo cheaper?

No. On the current indicative catalog rates Hailuo 02 Standard is listed at $0.575 per generation against $0.297 for Seedance V1 Lite. Runway Gen-4 at $0.069 is the cheaper listed route. Confirm live rates before production use.

Should I always switch from Seedance?

No. Keep Seedance when it wins the scene test.
