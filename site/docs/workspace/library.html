Your library

Where do the things I make go, and who can see them?

Everything you make in the workspace that is not text is kept: images and video clips, each with its prompt, the model that made it, the credits it cost and when. Conversations are not kept; Chat explains why.

Until ascian has copied a finished clip, and for up to 24 hours regardless, it also exists at a temporary address on the model gateway. Your library is yours to see and nobody else's: nothing in it is published, browsed or used for anything but showing it back to you. Ask and it is deleted. Privacy.
