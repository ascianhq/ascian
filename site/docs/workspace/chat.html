Chat

How does the workspace work, and what are its limits?

The workspace is the browser side of ascian: https://ascian.fun/chat. Sign in, pick a model, type. No key, no setup. Every model in the picker draws on the balance you funded.

How a turn works

  1. Pick a modelThe picker shows what one typical turn costs on each model (1,000 tokens in, 500 out), cheapest first. Switching keeps the conversation, so you can hand the same question to a second model.
  2. Type and sendThe reply streams in. Stop it early and you pay for what was generated, nothing after.
  3. See the costEach reply shows the credits it cost. The wallet lists every turn.

Limits

Messages kept
The last 20 are sent to the model, oldest dropped first to fit
Message size
48,000 characters
Conversation size
120,000 characters sent per turn; older turns fall off, the chat continues
Reply length
Up to 8,192 tokens per turn, thinking included; less if the balance cannot cover it
Pace
20 turns per minute
Deadline
4 minutes per reply

The API allows longer replies and conversations. When you outgrow the workspace, the CLI is the next step and takes a minute to set up.

Speak instead of typing, and hear the reply

A mic sits beside the paperclip in the composer. Tap it to listen and tap again to stop; sending stops it too. What you say is appended after what you typed, with the words still being guessed shown as they come and settled as you go, and a pause to think does not end the session. Four bars beside the mic move with your voice while it listens. Every finished reply has a read-aloud link that reads it in your browser's own voice: code is skipped with a word, links are read as their text, and one reading runs at a time.

Both run inside your browser. Chrome, Edge and Safari carry the speech engine, so the buttons appear there and stay hidden in Firefox. No audio is sent to ascian, nothing is recorded, and neither costs credits. The first tap asks for the microphone; if you have said no, the note tells you where the switch is on your platform.

What stays and what does not

Your chats are kept on your account, so they follow you across devices and are there when you come back. Each one is named by what it asks; the Chats tab of your account lists them and lets you download or delete any of them. Nobody at ascian reads them and nothing you type trains a model; the privacy page says exactly what is stored. Images and video clips you make are kept in your library.

If a model refuses to answer

A reply with no content is treated as an error, not an answer, and is not charged. A model whose provider is not currently serving it says so before the turn runs. Listed but refused. Every error in the workspace ends with a code; the list says what each one means.
