Holding ASCIAN

What holding the token does for you, and how it is measured.

Checked against pump.fun's published fees on 10 Oct 2026

Not financial advice, and not an offer. Nothing on these pages is an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any token or security, and it is not investment, financial, legal or tax advice. A token carries risk, including the risk of losing everything you put in. Do your own research and consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

Two things, both on one scale, both based on the share of total supply a linked wallet holds. Holding is enough: there is no staking and nothing to lock on your side.

A lower ascian fee

One rule: for every 1% of supply you hold, 25% off ascian's platform fee, capped at 100% at 4% of supply. It applies to ascian's fee only, never to the upstream model cost, so a 4% holder pays wholesale rather than nothing.

The platform fee is zero in the first version: ascian launches feeless and eats its own costs. When a fee is introduced it will be one published multiplier on top of upstream cost, and this discount scales it. Until then the discount changes nothing on your bill. Credits.

The scale is continuous for the discount and stepped for the bands. There are no tiers to argue about: the number is your share of supply.

How it is measured

  1. Link one wallet to your accountFrom the wallet page, sign a message with the wallet that holds your ASCIAN. No funds move and no transaction is sent; the signature proves the wallet is yours. One wallet per account.
  2. The balance is read on chainYour ASCIAN balance on Solana is read daily, and on demand from your account page. Your share of supply and your discount are shown there, along with the next band.
  3. The discount applies automaticallyWhen a platform fee exists, the discount is applied server-side on every request. The workspace price line shows it.
  • The company's locked 54,586,381 and the deployer wallet never count.
  • Only ASCIAN on Solana counts. Wrapped or bridged copies elsewhere do not.
  • Wallet linking and the bands ship after launch. This page will say the date when it is set.

Later

Two things under consideration once ascian has revenue, neither promised: committed model capacity for active API users, and a buyback rule with a published revenue trigger rather than a discretionary one. If either ships, it will be documented here before it applies.
