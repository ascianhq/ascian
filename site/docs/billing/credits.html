Credits

What is a credit, what is it worth, and what are the rules?

One credit
$0.001. One dollar buys 1,000 credits.
What it buys
Model usage, at the model's own rate. Nothing else.
Platform fee
None on usage today. A credit buys the same amount of model work it cost ascian. If that changes, the terms page announces it before it applies, and it never applies to credit you have already spent.
Processor fee on deposits
Absorbed by ascian today. You are credited for the full amount you paid; the processor's cut, about 1%, is a cost ascian carries. If that changes it is announced on the terms page first. Deposits.
Expiry
None. Credit stays until you use it.
Withdrawal
None. Credit is prepaid access to model usage and cannot be converted back to crypto or cash.
Interest or yield
None. Credit earns nothing.

Why credits and not dollars

Model prices are small fractions of a cent. A short chat turn on a cheap model costs a few hundredths of a credit, and a number like 0.032 reads better than $0.000032. Everything on the site, in the wallet and in ascian.credits_charged is in credits; the wallet shows the dollar equivalent beside the balance.

Where a deposit goes

The address is issued by NOWPayments, the payment processor. Your payment lands in ascian's balance there, and from it ascian sends the part that backs your credit on to the model gateway that runs the models. Each dollar you paid buys 1,000 credits; the processor's cut comes out of ascian's side, not yours.

When a platform fee is introduced

It will be one multiplier on top of upstream cost, shown as such, and holders of the ascian token pay less of it on a published scale. It will never touch the upstream price, and the docs and the terms will say the number.
