Questions, answered.

What is ASCIAN?

The ascian token: an SPL token on Solana. Holding it in a wallet linked to your account gives the account a lower ascian fee, new models first, priority routing and a say in the roadmap.

Does it pay a dividend or share revenue?

No, and it never will. ASCIAN buys access. It is not yield, not a revenue share, and holding it earns nothing. If you want a return, this is not that.

Is the supply capped?

Yes. 1,000,000,000 ASCIAN, fixed at creation, no mint function. Nothing was pre-sold or allocated.

What does the company hold?

5.46%, 54,586,381 ASCIAN, bought on the launch curve as the first trade. Locked in the PumpSwap pool: nothing for six months, then 22 equal releases of about 2,481,199 (0.248% of supply) every 30 days from 17 April 2027, the last on 6 January 2029. It never counts toward holder bands.

What is the contract address?

MINTNOTSETYET on Solana. Verify it on Solscan before you trade.

What happens to platform fees?

ascian charges no platform fee at launch. When one is introduced, holders pay less from the first day, per the rule above. The upstream model cost is always paid in full, so a 4% holder pays wholesale, not zero.

Are buybacks planned?

Off at launch. If they are ever turned on they follow a published rule, not a whim, and they would be announced here and in the room first.

Is the contract audited?

The launchpad, pump.fun, reports its contracts unaudited. The company's own exposure is the 5.46% it bought. Trade what you can afford to lose.
