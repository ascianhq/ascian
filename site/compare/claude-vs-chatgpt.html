Compare with your real context

Generic benchmark scores do not reveal how a model handles your contract, research pack or repository. Use the same source, constraints and expected format in both models.

A coding model also depends on context retrieval, tool access and how clearly it can inspect the codebase. Compare complete task success rather than an isolated snippet.
