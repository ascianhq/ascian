Price and what we can quote

Only one side of this comparison has a Ascian rate. Runway Gen-4 is listed at an indicative $0.069 per generation, and because the gateway bills per generation rather than per second, a longer clip does not multiply that figure. The number that moves a Runway budget is the number of attempts a shot needs. Confirm the live rate before production use.

Higgsfield has no route through the gateway today, so there is no catalog rate to set against Runway and we do not publish an estimate for one. Higgsfield's own published plan pricing is the authoritative source for that side, and it is market context rather than something a Ascian balance can spend.

02

Creative workflow

Higgsfield is compelling when the creator is thinking in directed shots and camera-led visual ideas. Runway is compelling when the video sits inside a wider production workflow. Today only Runway draws on the Ascian balance, so Higgsfield stays a separate account decision until it is routable.

03

How to choose

Start with a short test on the model that best matches the scene. Compare motion, subject consistency and the number of retries needed. Keep the winner for that shot, not necessarily for the entire project.

  • Choose Runway when you want a routable, per-generation cost you can see before running
  • Choose Higgsfield through Higgsfield when camera-led shot exploration is the requirement
  • Budget by generations attempted, not by seconds of finished footage

Higgsfield vs Runway FAQ

Is Higgsfield cheaper than Runway?

Ascian cannot answer that from its catalog, because Higgsfield has no gateway route and therefore no Ascian rate. Runway Gen-4 is billed per generation at an indicative $0.069. Any Higgsfield figure has to come from Higgsfield's own published pricing.

Can I use both without two subscriptions?

Not yet. Ascian makes supported models available through one universal balance, and Runway is one of them, but Higgsfield is not currently routable and remains a direct relationship.

Which is better for AI video?

The better model depends on the scene. Test the prompt against the routes available to you and compare usable output, not only the first generation.
