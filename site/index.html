    FAQ

    Everything you need to know: models, funding, privacy and what it costs.

    What is Ascian?

    Ascian is a single platform where you can access every major AI model for text, image, video and audio, paying only for what you use.

    Do I need a subscription?

    No. Ascian is pay as you go. You fund one balance and pay for the model usage you actually consume, with nothing recurring.

    How do I pay?

    You add credit with cryptocurrency. One dollar buys 1,000 credits, and model usage is metered against that single balance as you go. See pricing for the assets and networks accepted.

    What models are available?

    Models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek and xAI, alongside specialist image, video and audio providers. Browse the full catalogue.

    How much does it cost?

    One dollar buys 1,000 credits. Each request is metered against that balance at the model’s own rate, so a short chat costs a fraction of a credit while a video generation costs considerably more. There is no ascian fee at launch: a deposit credits in full, and the rate on the pricing page is the rate you pay. Estimate a workload.

    Is my data private?

    We do not use anything you type to train models. Your chats are kept on your account so they follow you between devices, and you can delete them at any time. Read the detail.

    Do my credits expire?

    No. Credit does not expire. It buys model usage only, earns nothing, and cannot be withdrawn.
