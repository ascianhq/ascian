pay for AI with crypto FAQ

Can Bitcoin pay for AI?

A service can accept supported Bitcoin funding and credit AI usage, subject to its available networks and terms.

Are AI credits a token?

Ascian credits are intended as product usage value, not a tradable cryptocurrency.

Should I send crypto to the MVP today?

Only when the live product explicitly provides a verified deposit flow. The current preview does not accept funds.
