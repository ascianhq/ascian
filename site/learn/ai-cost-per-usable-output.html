01

Use a simple production formula

Add the cost of successful and failed attempts. Divide by the number of accepted outputs. For team decisions, add editing time valued at an agreed internal rate.

Build the numerator in the unit the provider actually bills in. Image and video models on this gateway are charged per generation rather than per second of output, and language models are charged per token, so a media budget is the listed rate multiplied by attempts and not by the length of the finished clip.

02

Define usable before the test

Write the acceptance criteria before comparing models. Include factual accuracy, brand adherence, visual consistency, duration and export requirements as relevant.

03

Keep a lightweight test record

Record model version, prompt, settings, attempts and accepted outputs. This creates evidence that can survive a new model release.

AI cost per usable output FAQ

What is cost per usable output?

It is total attempt cost divided by outputs that meet the production standard.

Should editing time be included?

Include it when manual repair materially changes the comparison.

Why not compare list price only?

List price does not capture retries or failure rate, and it only means something once the billing unit is clear. Media models here are priced per generation, so the comparison is cost per accepted generation rather than cost per click.
