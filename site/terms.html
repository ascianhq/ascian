What the service is today

Ascian is early. Being clear about the gap matters more to us than looking further along than we are.

  • Working today: the public information pages, the model catalog, the calculators, email accounts, crypto funding, a credit balance, and a workspace with text chat, image generation and video generation. An API at /v1 with your own keys gives your own software the same chat models, including the ones that answer with images, and a command line tool that signs in with your account. API keys

You can create keys to use the models from your own software. A key is a credential that spends your balance, and anyone holding it can spend it: keep it out of anything you publish or commit, and revoke it in your wallet the moment you think it has escaped.

Usage through a key is yours. Requests made with it are charged to your balance whether you made them or something you wrote made them, and a script that repeats itself can spend a great deal very quickly. Setting a daily cap on a key is the control we give you for that, and we would rather you used it than found out the other way.

We show a key once and store only a hash of it, so we cannot recover one for you. If it is lost, revoke it and make another.

We may revoke a key ourselves where we reasonably believe it has been compromised, is being used to break the acceptable use rules below, or is doing something that threatens the service for other people.

Funding and how credit works

You add credit by sending cryptocurrency to an address issued for that one deposit. The assets we accept are the ones shown on your wallet page at the time, read live from our payment processor, and a deposit is between $5 and $10,000. One dollar that arrives buys 1,000 credits. Credit is then spent as you use models, priced at what the request actually cost us upstream, and the amount is shown against each request in your wallet.

The address is issued by NOWPayments, the payment processor we use. Your payment lands in our balance with them, and from there we send the part that backs your credit on to the model gateway that runs the models. What is credited to you follows what arrives, not what was quoted: the processor takes a small percentage for the payment and the conversion, at the moment about one percent, and the credit you receive is for the amount left after it.

  • A deposit address is issued to you, for that deposit. Do not reuse an old address and do not send to an address anyone gave you anywhere other than your signed in wallet page.
  • Send the asset on the network we name. Funds sent as the wrong asset, or on an unsupported network, or to a wrong address, are usually unrecoverable, and we cannot recover them for you.
  • Crypto transfers are irreversible. Once a transaction confirms on chain, neither you nor we can reverse it, and there is no chargeback.
  • Network fees are yours and are not credited, so send the quoted amount on top of whatever your wallet charges to send it.
  • The rate is the one quoted when the payment is created, not the rate displayed earlier and not the rate when it confirms.
  • A deposit address is good for a limited time, shown as a countdown next to it. Send within that window. Anything arriving after it may not reach your balance, and we may not be able to recover it.
  • Send the exact amount quoted. A payment short of it does not settle, so it does not credit.
  • Credit does not expire, earns nothing, and is not a claim on us for money.

We charge no platform fee on usage today: a credit buys the same amount of model work it cost us. If that changes, the change will be announced on this page before it applies, and it will never apply to credit you have already spent. A video render shows its exact price before you start it and puts that amount on hold until the render finishes. If the render fails, the hold is released and nothing is charged.

Credit is spend only. It buys model usage and it cannot be withdrawn, transferred to another person, or converted back into cryptocurrency or cash. There is no mechanism in the product that does so. This is deliberate: what you are buying is prepaid access to model usage, not a wallet we hold for you and not a deposit we owe you.

  • Where we are at fault, we will put it right. If a request was charged twice, charged for a reply you never received, or priced wrongly, tell us and we will correct your balance. That correction is made in credit.
  • Where we close or suspend your account for a reason that is not your fault, we will discuss returning the unspent portion with you.
  • We may decline a refund where we reasonably suspect fraud, sanctions evasion or abuse of the service, or where the law requires us to.

Nothing in this section removes rights you have as a consumer that cannot be waived by contract. Because we pay the upstream provider the moment you send a request, using the service is what consumes that right in respect of the credit you have already spent.
